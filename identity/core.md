# Who I am

## What I do

I'm Dimuthu, publishing as **Dimu Harshana**: a self-taught tech educator
and digital entrepreneur based in Sri Lanka. I teach motivated beginners to
become builders through WordPress fixes, Python automation (FastAPI), AI tools,
self-hosting and small digital products. Everything I publish comes from
hands-on experience. I run CeeVeeGlobal.com (tutorials + WordPress solutions),
AIBuiltTools.com (AI tools + digital store), and two YouTube channels:
"CeeVee Global" (WordPress/AI tutorials) and "Nature Lapse" (nature docs).

## What I'm working toward

- Turning learners into builders, step by step. That's the mission.
- A content machine where one idea runs blog → video → social, each stage
  seeded from real evidence in the one before, and I hold the approval gate.
- Selling what I build: PDFs, eBooks, Python apps, AI tools.
- Using Claude as an actual developer on my sites, not as a chatbot.

## How I got here

15+ years in IT, and 8+ years specialising in WordPress, automation and AI
tools. CeeVeeGlobal grew messy over years of PHP snippets and a point system
nobody needed, and got very slow. In May 2026 I started rebuilding it with
Claude Code doing the heavy lifting. I speak from things I've built and
broken, so read my lessons as experience and not theory.

## Cross-cutting principles

- **Teach what I test.** If I haven't run it, I don't teach it.
- **Start simple. Build fast. Learn by doing.** Smallest working thing first.
- **AI does the work; I approve it.** Agents draft and propose. Nothing is
  marked approved or goes live unless I press the button myself.

## What I don't do

- Preach from a mountaintop. I'm a peer who's slightly ahead, not a guru.
- Hype, generic authority voice, or pushy CTAs.
- Hide the missteps. What didn't work stays in.
- Pay for an API or tool when a free, local or self-hosted option does the job.
- Write monolithic code. One job per script, one thing per plugin.
