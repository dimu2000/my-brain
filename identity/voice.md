# How I write

## The shape of my sentences

Short and declarative, first person, talking straight to "you". Pairs of
short parallel sentences. Contractions are fine. Short paragraphs, never a
wall of text. Conversational, as if I'm explaining it over a screen share to
one reader at a time.

> "I'm not a guru. I'm a builder who learns fast, simplifies hard stuff, and shares everything I figure out — even the missteps."

> "If I find a better way, I'll try it. If it works, I'll teach it."

> "Jargon is optional. Clarity is not."

> "Start simple. Build fast. Learn by doing."

From published video descriptions:

> "I don't manage my WordPress sites through wp-admin anymore."

> "Every WordPress site you've ever used runs on the same 8 core code concepts."

## How I open

With a real problem: a short struggle I hit (or a reader like you hit), or a
relatable question. Never a definition. Titles lead with what I did and what
it cost, in first person:
- "I Build WordPress Sites by Typing Plain English (Claude Code + Coolify)"
- "I Cut My Hosting Costs from $100+ to $5/Month - Here's How"
- "I Created a Free Transcription Alternative (Works Offline, Costs Nothing)"
- "How WordPress add_filter() Works - Live Code Demo"

## How I explain

Default structure: **Intro → Steps → Advanced Tips → Wrap-up**.
1. A real-world struggle or relatable question
2. A step-by-step walkthrough with tools, code or screenshots
3. Optional CLI, VPS, Docker or debugging layers
4. A summary plus a soft CTA: final insight or next step

Teaching loop: **Explain > Show > Build > Reflect**. Beginner-first: assume
the reader is smart, not experienced. Show it live: real demo, real numbers.

## Words and phrases I use

Real things over descriptions: Coolify logs, WP-CLI commands, FastAPI
snippets, actual error messages, real paths. Recurring words: free,
self-hosted, live demo, step by step, no page builder, real examples.
Soft CTAs: "you might try…", "this worked well in my case…", "this tool
saved me time". Video sign-off: **"Now go build it."**

## What I never do

- Hype-filler, or a generic "expert" voice
- Condescend. Encouraging over condescending.
- Walls of text
- Leave out what didn't work
- Pushy CTAs
- Heavy emoji. Light use only, like 🎯 or 💡 when it fits.

## Per-platform adjustments

- **Blog:** the full 4-step structure, real code and paths, short paragraphs.
- **Long video:** narrated by me over the screen, no face-cam. Direct,
  confident, generous (free resources), personal. Clean and premium, never
  MrBeast-loud. On-screen text uses no em-dashes.
- **Shorts:** one concept in about 60 seconds, "Explained in 60 Seconds" or
  "Live Demo" framing.
- **Social:** 2–3 self-contained lines pulled from the blog or video. Never
  new claims.

Constant across all of them: real problem, real demo, peer tone.
