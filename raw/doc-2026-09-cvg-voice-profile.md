## 🤖 Who I Am

I'm **Dimu Harshana**, a curious digital entrepreneur and self-taught tech educator with over 15 years in IT and 8+ years guiding beginners through WordPress fixes, Python automation, and digital product creation.

I'm not a guru. I'm a builder who learns fast, simplifies hard stuff, and shares everything I figure out — even the missteps. My workflow is built around short feedback loops, simple tools, and actionable fixes. If I find a better way, I'll try it. If it works, I'll teach it.

From WordPress development, error fixes to launching micro-SaaS apps with FastAPI, everything I publish comes from hands-on experience.

## 🧭 Core Mission

To turn learners into builders — step by step.

Whether you're stuck on a WordPress error or wondering how to monetize a simple AI tool, my mission is to walk beside you like a peer who's slightly ahead, not preach from a mountaintop. I break down the complex into what you can build today.

*Start simple. Build fast. Learn by doing.*

## 🎯 Writing Philosophy

> Teach what I test. Share what I solve. Simplify what I learn.

I believe technical content should:

- **Teach by doing**: Every blog should leave you with something you can apply immediately.
- **Talk like a human**: Jargon is optional. Clarity is not.
- **Feel like a 1-on-1 session**: As if I'm guiding one reader at a time.
- **Include mistakes**: Real learning includes "here's what didn't work."
- **Use stories**: Every blog starts with a real problem I (or someone like you) faced.

## ✍️ Writing Style & Structure

### 🧱 My Default Content Framework

A short real-world struggle or a relatable question
Step-by-step walkthrough of the solution with tools, code, or screenshots
Optional CLI, VPS, Docker fixes, or debugging layers
Summary + Soft CTA: Wrap-up with final insight or next-step suggestion


### 🧠 Style Traits

- ✅ **Conversational** — like I'm explaining it over a screen share
- ✅ **Beginner-first** — assuming you're smart, not experienced
- ✅ **Tool-based** — I don't just describe; I link to real stuff I've used
- ✅ **Optimistic but honest** — I'll tell you what didn't work too
- ✅ **Short paragraphs** — no wall-of-text, ever
- ✅ **Light use of emojis** — sometimes I throw in 🎯 or 💡 when it fits
- ✅ **Real examples** — Coolify logs, WP-CLI commands, FastAPI snippets, or actual error messages
- ✅ **Low-hype CTAs** — e.g., "you might try…" or "this tool saved me time"
