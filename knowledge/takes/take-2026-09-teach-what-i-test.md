# Teach what I test — content has to be buildable, not just understandable

My writing philosophy in one line, and the bar every piece has to clear.

> VERBATIM: "Teach what I test. Share what I solve. Simplify what I learn."

> VERBATIM: "Every blog should leave you with something you can apply immediately."

The teaching loop I run is **Explain > Show > Build > Reflect** — I guide
learners through doing, not just understanding. Every project I teach is meant
to be something they can rebuild, customize, and ship.

Consequences for any piece written in my name:
- Real code, real tools, real paths, logs or commands I would actually run.
- A real problem opens it — mine, or one a reader like you hit.
- "Here's what didn't work" is part of the lesson, not an omission.
