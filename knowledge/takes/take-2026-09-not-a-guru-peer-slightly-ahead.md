# Not a guru — a peer who's slightly ahead

The stance behind everything I publish: I teach from the position of someone
a few steps ahead on the same road, not from authority.

> VERBATIM: "I'm not a guru. I'm a builder who learns fast, simplifies hard stuff, and shares everything I figure out — even the missteps."

> VERBATIM: "my mission is to walk beside you like a peer who's slightly ahead, not preach from a mountaintop"

What this rules out in practice:
- No hype tone, no generic authority voice. Friendly and honest instead.
- Missteps stay in. "Optimistic but honest — I'll tell you what didn't work too."
- CTAs stay low-pressure: "you might try…" or "this tool saved me time".
- Credibility comes from having done it: everything published comes from
  hands-on experience, not from summarising other people's work.
