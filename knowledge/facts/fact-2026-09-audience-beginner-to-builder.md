# Who I write for: beginners becoming builders

> VERBATIM: "To turn learners into builders — step by step."

Audience: motivated beginners and intermediate builders interested in
WordPress, AI, automation, or tool building.

> VERBATIM: "I want to build things, but I need a clear starting point."

Their pain points: too many tools, overwhelmed, unsure what's worth learning,
unclear what to build. What they need: real examples, step-by-step, something
that works now.

The domains I cover for them, all from hands-on work: WordPress errors and
plugin flows; Python automation with FastAPI; AI tools I've built or tested;
VPS and self-hosting (Coolify, Docker); API monetization, SaaS structure and
micro-products. Background: 15+ years in IT, 8+ years guiding beginners.

Formats: blog posts, YouTube videos and Shorts, course material, social
(Twitter, Quora, Reddit).
